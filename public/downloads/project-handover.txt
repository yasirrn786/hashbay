HASHBAY PROJECT HANDOVER CHECKLIST
Agree the applicable items in the project scope. Not every project needs every item.

A. Ownership
Customer account and repository owners recorded
Source files and configuration delivered as agreed
Pre-existing and third-party components identified with licence terms
Temporary access and credential transfer handled securely

B. Operation
Dependencies and versions listed
Deployment instructions tested by someone who did not build the system
Operating guide and escalation contact documented
Backup/restore procedure tested where included
Known issues and residual dependencies recorded

C. Acceptance
Named acceptance tests run and evidence recorded
Customer walkthrough scheduled
Responsible customer contact confirms receipt
Failed tests resolved or recorded before sign-off

D. Exit and support
Temporary access revoked
Service hours and separately agreed support period confirmed
Renewals, recurring costs and exit responsibilities recorded
Open tasks assigned to a named owner

Do not put secrets in public documentation.
