HASHBAY QUOTE COMPARISON CHECKLIST
Compare the same scope, not just the headline price.

For each supplier record:
1. Scope and quantities: users, devices, systems, included tasks and hours
2. Service window and contact method
3. Response target, resolution dependencies and escalation owner
4. Remote work and separately confirmed on-site coverage
5. Labour and onboarding
6. Hardware, spare parts and damage exclusions
7. Licences, platform subscriptions and third-party charges
8. Travel and location-specific costs
9. Taxes and total payable
10. One-time versus recurring charges
11. Approved overage rates and change process
12. Acceptance, handover, support duration and exit charges
13. Ownership of accounts, source files and licensed components
14. Cancellation and payment milestones
15. Availability of the delivery lead and any specialist

Unknown systems may need a paid assessment before a fixed price.
This is a discussion worksheet, not a price list or contract.
